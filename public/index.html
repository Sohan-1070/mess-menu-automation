How to run 24/7 when your Laptop and Phone are switched OFF

By default, running on your laptop stops when the laptop shuts down. To have this run completely autonomously in the background 365 days a year without needing your computer on, deploy this app to a Free 24/7 Cloud Host.

1. Does your phone need to stay ON?

NO! Because WhatsApp uses the modern Multi-Device protocol, once you scan the QR code to link the bot, WhatsApp grants an independent secure session. Your phone can be out of battery, switched off, or disconnected from the internet, and WhatsApp will still deliver the messages from the server.

2. Does your laptop need to stay ON?

Not if you host it in the Cloud! We have pre-configured this app with a Dockerfile and render.yaml so you can deploy it to Render.com or Railway.app in 3 minutes for free. The cloud server stays alive 24/7 in a data center.

3-Minute Free Cloud Deployment (Render.com)

1
Push this project to a free GitHub repository

Initialize git and push the files in this folder to your GitHub account (public or private).

2
Sign up on Render.com & click "New Web Service"

Select your GitHub repository. Build Command: npm install, Start Command: node server.js.

3
Open your live URL (e.g. https://my-mess-bot.onrender.com)

Scan the QR code once with your phone, select your WhatsApp group, and drop your monthly Excel sheet. It will send notifications every day automatically, even when your phone and laptop are off!
